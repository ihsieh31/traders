# AI-Berkshire adaptation provenance

This repository's formal A/B backend adapts the analysis-stage ideas from:

- Source repository: `xbtlin/ai-berkshire`
- Source commit: `1cc1e362378cd3fea99a4f4c3b50676bce9aa4c6`
- Adapted concepts: `investment-team`, `investment-research`, and the
  `financial_rigor` methodology.

The adaptation is intentionally limited to four frozen-evidence research roles
(`business_analyst`, `financial_analyst`, `industry_researcher`, and
`risk_assessor`) followed by a non-executable Team Lead synthesis.  It retains
business essence, moat, inversion, management quality, industry/civilization
trend, valuation/margin of safety, explicit uncertainty, missing-data
discipline, and cross-validation before confidence.

Intentionally excluded from this A/B backend are portfolio review, industry
funnel, quality screen, thesis tracker, news pulse, earnings team, portfolio
sizing, trade execution, and final buy/sell recommendations.  Those would
change the controlled variable or bypass Traders' shared downstream decision
system.

The source project's license and attribution requirements must be preserved
when substantial source code is copied.  This implementation re-expresses the
small role/schema/calculation surface locally and keeps the source provenance
here for future review.
